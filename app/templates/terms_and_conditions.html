Terms and Conditions (T&C)

Last Updated: January 2026

By using the Placement Portal, all Students and Companies agree to abide by the following Code of Conduct. Failure to comply may result in account termination or legal action.

  1. Professional Respect: All users must maintain professional respect toward participating companies and the institution.
  2. Intent to Hire/Work: Users should only register for job positions if they have a genuine willingness to fulfill the role requirements.
  3. Data Integrity: Any fake or fraudulent details discovered in a profile will lead to the immediate termination of the account from our platform.
  4. Violation Reporting: Any violation complained of by a company shall be investigated and treated by our dedicated support team.
  5. Disciplinary Action: Action will be taken according to the severity of the violation. This is not limited to account termination but may also include legal proceedings.
  6. Code of Conduct: Both companies and students are required to maintain a strictly professional code of conduct at all times during the recruitment process.
  7. Impartiality: Companies must conduct interviews for all shortlisted students with strict impartiality and no partiality.
  8. Anti-Exploitation: No company shall demand any monetary amount from students under the guise of "fees." Such actions will lead to disciplinary measures and potential court fines.
  9. Anti-Bribery Policy: No student shall offer bribes, gifts, or any other form of illegal gratification to company recruiters.
  10. Report Threshold: A student is permitted a maximum of 3 reports from 3 different companies. If the report count exceeds this threshold, the student's account will be terminated immediately.
